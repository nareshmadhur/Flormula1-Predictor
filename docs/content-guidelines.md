# Content Guidelines

## Goal

Keep public product copy clear, confident, and user-facing.

Roadmap notes, implementation details, and legal/compliance caveats belong in docs or admin surfaces, not on public website pages.

## Public Page Rules

- Write for visitors, members, and group organizers.
- Explain what users can do now.
- Keep copy short and action-oriented.
- Use friendly words like `group`, `pool`, `picks`, `standings`, and `recap`.
- Keep disclaimers concise and factual.
- Credit external services only where useful for trust.
- Keep future plans, launch notes, and build rationale in roadmap docs.

## Avoid On Public Pages

- `draft status`
- `roadmap`
- `planned`
- `legal review`
- `commercialization`
- `implementation`
- `RLS`
- `database`
- `storage rules`
- `technical components`
- explanations of why something is not built yet
- future-facing sections such as `What comes next`

## Where These Notes Belong

- Product plans: `docs/roadmap`
- Engineering details: `README.md` or architecture docs
- Operational caveats: admin pages
- Public trust statements: footer, privacy, terms, about

## Good Examples

Use:

- `Flormula1 is built for friendly private prediction groups.`
- `Pick the podium before FP1, then follow the standings after the race.`
- `Want to run a private group? Email support and we will help you get started.`

Avoid:

- `This page is a draft and needs legal review.`
- `The commercial roadmap includes reminder preferences.`
- `A contact form would require spam protection and RLS.`
- `What comes next: invite links, reminders, and organizer tools.`
